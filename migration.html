❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about migration.

How long does the migration process take? +

Most applications are reviewed within 24-48 hours. Once approved, you can join the alliance immediately. The full migration (moving your account) depends on the game's mechanics, but we'll guide you through every step.

Do I lose my progress when migrating? +

No! Your account progress, power, troops, and items stay with you. Migration just means changing alliances. You keep everything you've built.

What is the Welcome Package? +

The Welcome Package includes resource packs, speed-ups, and other in-game items to help you rebuild faster in your new home. Exact contents vary based on your power level.

Can I migrate with my friends? +

Absolutely! We love group migrations. Each person still needs to fill out an application, but mention in the form that you're migrating together. We'll coordinate your arrival.

What if my application is rejected? +

We'll provide feedback on why it was rejected. Most rejections are due to incomplete info or not meeting minimum requirements. You can reapply after addressing the issues.

Is there a trial period? +

Yes! New members have a 7-day trial period where both you and we can ensure it's a good fit. After that, you're officially part of the LCKY family.
